Shipping Policy

Everything you need to know about how and when your order arrives.

1. Shipping Area

We currently ship inside Egypt only.

2. Processing Time

Orders are processed within 1–3 business days.

3. Delivery Time

  • Cairo & Giza: 2–6 business days

4. Shipping Fees

Shipping fees are calculated based on the customer's location and are displayed at checkout.

5. Order Refusal

Customers have the right to refuse the order upon delivery before the courier leaves. In this case, the customer is responsible for paying the shipping fees only.

Thank you for shopping with NUVANTI. We are committed to providing you with the best quality and service.
