About Nuvanti

Fewer things, made properly.

Our Story

Started with a polo we couldn't buy anywhere else.

Nuvanti began with a knitted polo — contrast tipping, a hand-embroidered signature, a fit that didn't lose its shape after a few wears. We couldn't find one built the way we wanted, so we made it ourselves.

That polo became the starting point for everything since: the embroidered tanks, the wide-leg sweatpants, each one built around the same small details worked by hand rather than printed on.

Design Philosophy

Proportion first, decoration last.

We design around how a piece sits on a body in motion, not how it photographs flat. Logos stay small or disappear entirely. Colors are chosen to work across a wardrobe, not to stand out in a single outfit.

We release fewer collections than most brands our size — usually three or four a year — because getting a fit right takes longer than a monthly drop calendar allows.

Quality

Built to outlast the season it was made for.

We keep every style in ribbed knits and heavyweight cottons that hold their shape wash after wash, and every embroidery — the script signature, the star crest, the graffiti patch — is worked directly into the fabric rather than printed on top of it. We stand behind it with a 14-day exchange window on every order.
