Exchange & Refund Policy

Please read this carefully before your order arrives.

1. Try-On at the Door

You are allowed to try the item when the courier arrives. If you do not want it, you may return the entire package and pay the applicable shipping fees.

2. Exchange Policy

We offer exchange only within 14 days from the delivery date.

Conditions for exchange:

  • Item must be unused.
  • Item must be in original condition and packaging.
  • Original invoice must be provided.
  • Customers are responsible for paying shipping fees for exchanges unless the item has a manufacturing defect.

3. Refund Policy

Refunds are not available.

You can check your order while the courier is at your door — if it fits, keep it. If not, please return it immediately to the courier, as returns are not accepted afterward.

In case of a manufacturing defect:

  • The item must be reported within 48 hours of receiving the order.
  • Proof (photos or video) may be required.
  • If approved, the customer may receive a replacement or refund.

4. Non-Returnable Cases

  • Used or washed items
  • Damaged items caused by misuse
  • Requests made after 14 days

Thank you for shopping with NUVANTI. We are committed to providing you with the best quality and service.
