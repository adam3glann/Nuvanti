Our Approach

Small details, worked by hand.

Every Nuvanti piece starts with the embroidery — the script signature, the star crest, the graffiti-style patch — worked directly onto ribbed knits and heavyweight cotton rather than printed on top of them.

We keep the catalog small on purpose: a handful of styles, each offered in the colors people actually reach for, restocked rather than replaced every season.
