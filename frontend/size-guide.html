How to Measure

Chest: Measure across the garment from armpit to armpit, then double it.

Length: Measure from the highest point of the shoulder seam straight down to the hem.

Waist: Measure straight across the waistband from edge to edge, then double it.

Fit Notes

Most Nuvanti tops are designed with a relaxed, slightly oversized fit — if you're between sizes and prefer a closer fit, size down. Bottoms run true to size.
